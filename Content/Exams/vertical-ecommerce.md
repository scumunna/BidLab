- It is $80, because only 75% of acquired customers are retained, so $60 / 0.75 = $80, and CM2 must be computed on net revenue.
- It is $45, because the 25% return rate reduces the cost proportionally.

explain: Re-gross CAC for retention: 60 / (1 - 0.25) = 60 / 0.75 = 80. A "profitable" $60 CAC is really $80 once a quarter of orders come back, and CM2 must run on net revenue, not gross orders, or returns erase the contribution a healthy ROAS implied.

:::q Amazon Efficiency: ACoS, TACoS, and the Flywheel
question: A Sponsored Products line spends $5,000, drives $20,000 in ad-attributed sales, and the listing also books $30,000 in organic sales. Compute ACoS and TACoS, and state which one shows whether ads are building organic momentum.
- ACoS is 25% and TACoS is 10%; TACoS reveals whether organic is carrying more of the listing.
- ACoS is 10% and TACoS is 25%; ACoS reveals the organic momentum.
- ACoS is 25% and TACoS is 25%; the two are always equal.
- ACoS is 16.7% and TACoS is 10%; ACoS reveals the organic momentum.
answer: 0
explain: ACoS = 5,000 / 20,000 = 25%. TACoS = 5,000 / (20,000 + 30,000) = 10%. TACoS divides by total sales, so a falling TACoS at flat or rising total sales is the signal that organic is carrying the listing; ACoS is blind to organic by construction.
